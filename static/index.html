Top 10 — Form

Last 30 days
What is form? A rolling 30-day average of FPL points. Scores above 6.0 are excellent — these players are in red-hot shape right now. Form fades quickly for injured or rested players, so chase current form over past reputation.

Top 10 — Value Score

180+ mins played
What is value score? Total Points ÷ Price (£m). A score above 4.0 means strong returns per pound spent. Only players with 180+ minutes are shown to remove players with lucky one-off scores. These are your budget gems.

Top 10 — Season Points

All gameweeks
Why it matters: Consistent high scorers over the season are reliable captaincy options. Note that one big haul can inflate a player's total — check if points came from one gameweek or spread across many before captaining.

Top 10 — xGI

Expected involvements
What is xGI? Expected Goal Involvements = xG + xA. It measures chance quality and key passes, regardless of whether the player converted. A high xGI player who hasn't scored yet may be "due" — they're called differentials and can be low-ownership picks.
